I'm Mfecho Jerase, the founder of bynUp. With a degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, I've always been passionate about using technology to solve real-world problems. bynUp was born from a simple observation: too many entrepreneurs and small businesses struggle to establish their online presence due to complex, expensive website builders.

Based in Bamenda, Cameroon, I built bynUp to serve businesses everywhere—from local shops to international brands. The internet knows no borders, and neither do we.

As a solo founder, I personally oversee every aspect of the platform. When you use bynUp, you're not just another customer—you're part of a mission to make professional website creation accessible to everyone, regardless of where they're located or what budget they're working with. Every feature is built with care, every support request gets my personal attention, and every success story motivates me to keep improving.
