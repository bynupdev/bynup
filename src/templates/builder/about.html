bynUp exists for the merchants the major platforms forgot — no matter where you live, what bank you use, or how big your business is.

Mfecho Jerase

Founder & CEO
Bamenda, Cameroon Electrical & Electronics Engineering

I'm Mfecho Jerase, the founder of bynUp. With a degree in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, I've always been drawn to problems that other people have given up on. bynUp started from one I kept running into: brilliant entrepreneurs all over the world being locked out of ecommerce — not because they lacked a product, but because the platforms that claimed to serve "everyone" quietly didn't.

Stripe still doesn't work in dozens of countries. PayPal holds funds for weeks in others. Card networks charge merchants in emerging markets fees that eat their entire margin. The tools that promise global reach are, in practice, built for a handful of countries and everyone else learns to work around it.

So I built bynUp differently — on rails that don't have borders. That's why we run on crypto: it works the same in Bamenda, Lagos, Karachi, and São Paulo as it does in San Francisco. Same speed, same cost, no permission required. It's not a compromise — it's the entire point.

As a solo founder I personally oversee every part of the platform. When you use bynUp, you're not a support ticket — you're part of a mission to open ecommerce to everyone the old systems left behind. Every feature is built with that in mind, and every merchant who launches a store here proves the thesis a little more.

Our Mission

To make professional ecommerce accessible to every entrepreneur on the planet — regardless of technical skill, location, or which bank happens to support them. A great storefront shouldn't depend on a merchant's passport.

Our Vision

A world where starting an online business is a matter of will, not geography. We're building the infrastructure so that the next million merchants come from places the last million never had a chance.

Why we accept crypto

We chose the payment rail that doesn't exclude anyone.

Our subscriptions run on crypto — not because it's trendy, but because it is the only payment method that works everywhere our customers do. If you're in a country Stripe doesn't serve, or your business can't get a merchant account, you can still pay us and get online in seconds.
