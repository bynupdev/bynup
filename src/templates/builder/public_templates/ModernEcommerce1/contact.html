Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my delivery schedule?
You can easily modify your delivery schedule by logging into your account and navigating to the "Delivery Schedule" section. Changes must be made by Wednesday at 11:59 PM EST for the following week's delivery.
What if I'm not home for delivery?
No worries! Our meals are packaged with ice packs to stay fresh for several hours. You can also provide delivery instructions for a safe spot to leave your box.
Can I customize my meal preferences?
Absolutely! You can set dietary preferences, allergies, and ingredient dislikes in your account settings. Our system will automatically filter meal options to match your preferences.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can pause or cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard. There are no cancellation fees or commitments.
What areas do you deliver to?
We currently deliver to all 50 states in the US. Enter your zip code during signup to confirm availability in your area.
Are your ingredients organic?
We prioritize organic ingredients whenever possible. Approximately 85% of our produce is certified organic, and all our meats are antibiotic-free.
