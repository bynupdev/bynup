From Humble Beginnings

FreshPlate started in 2013 with a simple idea: make restaurant-quality meals accessible to busy families and individuals. Our founder, Sarah Chen, a former executive chef, noticed how many people wanted to cook delicious, healthy meals but struggled with meal planning, grocery shopping, and recipe complexity.

What began as a small operation serving 50 families in New York City has grown into the nation's leading meal delivery service, serving millions of customers across all 50 states. Despite our growth, we've stayed true to our original mission: delivering chef-designed recipes and fresh ingredients that make home cooking easy and enjoyable.

Today, our team of 50+ chefs continues to innovate, creating new recipes each week while maintaining the quality and consistency that our customers have come to expect.
