Meet Our Team

From Passion to Purpose

Founded by Sarah Bennett, a mother of three with a background in textile design, {brand} was born from a personal need. After struggling to find truly comfortable and beautiful clothing for her own children, Sarah decided to create her own line.

What started as a small studio in her home has grown into a beloved brand trusted by thousands of families worldwide. Our commitment remains the same: to provide parents with clothing they can feel good about and babies can feel comfortable in.

Today, we partner with ethical manufacturers who share our values of sustainability and craftsmanship, ensuring every garment meets our high standards of quality and responsibility.
