Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions

What is your shipping policy?

+

We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $50 within the continental US. Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days, while expedited shipping (2-day) is available for an additional fee. International shipping is available to select countries with delivery times varying by location.

What is your return policy?

+

We offer a 30-day return policy for most items in original condition with packaging. Some items may have different return windows or conditions (noted on product pages). Returns are free for defective items, while non-defective returns may incur a restocking fee. Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days after we receive your return.

Do you offer technical support?

+

Yes! We offer comprehensive technical support for all products purchased from TechSphere. You can contact our support team via phone, email, or live chat during business hours. We also provide online resources including setup guides, troubleshooting articles, and video tutorials on our support portal.

What payment methods do you accept?

+

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay. All payments are processed securely using industry-standard encryption. We do not store your payment information on our servers.

Do you offer international shipping?

+

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination. Customs duties and taxes may apply depending on your country's regulations and are the responsibility of the recipient. You can view available countries and estimated shipping costs at checkout.
