Frequently Asked Questions

What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy for all items in original condition. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Return shipping is free for orders over $100. Please contact our support team to initiate a return.
How long does shipping take?
Orders typically ship within 1-2 business days. Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days within the US. Express shipping (1-2 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee. International shipping times vary by destination.
Do you offer international shipping?
Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination. Customs fees and import duties are the responsibility of the customer.
How do I track my order?
Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can also track your order by logging into your account and viewing your order history.
Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes, we offer complimentary gift wrapping on all orders. You can add a personalized gift message at checkout. Each item is beautifully packaged in our signature gift box.
