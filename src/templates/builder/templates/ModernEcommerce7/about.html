Our Story

Crafting timeless luxury with passion, precision, and purpose since 2010

The Art of Luxury Beauty

Founded in the heart of Paris, Luxe Essence began as a vision to redefine luxury cosmetics and fragrances. Our journey started with a simple belief: true luxury lies in exceptional quality, sustainable practices, and unforgettable experiences.

Today, we continue to honor our heritage while innovating for the future, creating products that celebrate individuality and timeless beauty.

Our Mission & Values

Excellence in Craftsmanship

Every product is meticulously crafted by master perfumers and cosmetic scientists who share our commitment to perfection.

Sustainable Luxury

We source ethically and sustainably, ensuring our luxury doesn't compromise the wellbeing of our planet or communities.

Authentic Connection

We build genuine relationships with our customers, understanding that luxury is as much about experience as it is about product.

Innovation & Heritage

We blend time-honored techniques with cutting-edge technology to create products that are both classic and contemporary.

Our Journey

2010

The Beginning

Founded in Paris by Isabella Moreau, Luxe Essence launched with our first fragrance collection, inspired by the gardens of Versailles.

2013

Expansion

We expanded our offerings to include a luxury cosmetics line, starting with our signature Velvet Noir lipstick collection.

2016

Sustainability Commitment

Implemented our comprehensive sustainability program, achieving carbon neutrality and 100% recyclable packaging.

2020

Global Recognition

Received the prestigious Luxury Beauty Award for Innovation and Sustainability, marking our arrival on the global stage.

2023

Digital Transformation

Launched our enhanced digital experience, bringing the Luxe Essence atelier to customers worldwide through virtual consultations.

Our Sustainability Commitment

Circular Packaging

100% of our packaging is recyclable or refillable, with innovative programs to reduce waste throughout our supply chain.

Ethical Sourcing

We partner with certified sustainable farms and cooperatives worldwide, ensuring fair wages and environmental stewardship.

Carbon Neutral Operations

All our facilities are powered by renewable energy, and we offset 100% of our carbon emissions through certified programs.

Clean Formulations

Our products are formulated without harmful chemicals, prioritizing natural, biodegradable ingredients whenever possible.
