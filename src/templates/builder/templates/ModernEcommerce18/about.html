Our Story

Crafting premium pet essentials with love, care, and unwavering commitment to quality since 2015.

A Passion Born from Love

PŌTA was founded in 2015 by Emma Bennett, a devoted pet parent who believed that our companions deserve the very best. After struggling to find products that combined luxury aesthetics with genuine comfort and durability, Emma decided to create her own collection.

What began as a small atelier in Brooklyn has grown into a beloved brand trusted by pet parents worldwide. Today, we continue to honor Emma's original vision: to create beautiful, functional products that enhance the lives of pets and their families.

"Every product we create is a reflection of the love we have for our own companions. Quality, comfort, and style should never be compromised." - Emma Bennett, Founder

Our Philosophy

Uncompromising Quality

We source only the finest materials and subject every product to rigorous quality testing. Your pet deserves nothing less than excellence.

Artisan Craftsmanship

Each piece is thoughtfully designed and meticulously crafted by skilled artisans who share our passion for excellence.

Conscious Compassion

We are committed to ethical sourcing, sustainable practices, and giving back to animal welfare organizations.

Our Mission

To create heirloom-quality pet essentials that celebrate the bond between humans and their animal companions, combining timeless design with exceptional craftsmanship while maintaining ethical and sustainable practices throughout our supply chain.

The Art of Craftsmanship

Meticulous Attention to Detail

Every PŌTA product begins as a sketch, meticulously refined to ensure both beauty and functionality. Our designers work closely with veterinarians and pet behaviorists to understand what truly matters for your companion's comfort and well-being.

From selecting premium, non-toxic materials to testing durability against the most enthusiastic play, we leave no detail overlooked. Each product undergoes multiple quality checks before it reaches your doorstep.

Our Journey Through Time

2015

A Dream Takes Shape

Emma Bennett founded PŌTA in her Brooklyn apartment, creating the first collection of luxury pet beds by hand. The brand quickly gains a devoted following among local pet parents.

2018

Expanding the Vision

PŌTA launches its full product line, including grooming essentials, dining accessories, and travel gear. The first flagship boutique opens in SoHo, New York.

2020

A Commitment to Sustainability

PŌTA transitions to 100% sustainable packaging and partners with ethical suppliers committed to environmental responsibility. The brand also launches its first charitable initiative, donating 5% of profits to animal rescue organizations.

2023

Global Recognition

PŌTA expands internationally, serving pet parents in over 30 countries. The brand is recognized for its innovative designs and commitment to animal welfare, receiving multiple industry awards.

Present Day

Continuing the Legacy

Today, PŌTA continues to innovate, creating thoughtfully designed products that enhance the lives of pets and their families. Our mission remains the same: to celebrate the bond we share with our animal companions through quality, style, and care.
