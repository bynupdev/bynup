Our Story

Redefining gaming gear through innovation, precision, and passion since 2018

Born from Passion, Built for Performance

NEXUS was founded by a team of competitive gamers and engineers who believed that gaming gear should be as dedicated to performance as the players themselves. What started in a small garage in San Francisco has grown into a global brand trusted by esports athletes and casual gamers alike.

Our mission is simple: create precision-crafted peripherals that give you the competitive edge. Every product we design undergoes rigorous testing to ensure it meets the demands of professional gaming.

"Gear is an extension of the player. When it disappears in your hands, that's when magic happens." — Marcus Chen, Founder

Our Journey

2018
The Beginning
NEXUS is founded in San Francisco with a mission to revolutionize gaming peripherals. First prototype of the Phantom keyboard is created.
2019
First Launch
Phantom Mech Keyboard launches to critical acclaim, selling out within 48 hours. NEXUS gains recognition in the gaming community.
2020
Expansion
Aether X Wireless Mouse and Spectra Headset join the lineup. NEXUS becomes official sponsor of multiple esports teams.
2022
Global Recognition
NEXUS products are featured in major gaming tournaments worldwide. Awarded "Best Gaming Peripherals" by TechGaming Magazine.
2024
Sustainable Future
Commit to 100% carbon neutral operations and eco-friendly packaging. Launch of the Pro Series for competitive esports.
Today
Global Community
Serving over 1 million gamers across 50+ countries, continuing to innovate and push the boundaries of gaming technology.
