Frequently Asked Questions

How do I determine my ring size?

We recommend visiting a local jeweler for the most accurate measurement. Alternatively, you can use our printable ring sizer available on our website or purchase a ring sizer kit from us. For reference, the average women's ring size is 6-7, and men's is 9-10.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for all unworn jewelry in its original condition with all certificates and packaging. Custom pieces and engraved items cannot be returned. Returns are processed within 5-7 business days, and you will receive a full refund to your original payment method.

Do you offer custom design services?

Yes, we specialize in custom jewelry design. Our process begins with a consultation (in-person or virtual) to discuss your vision, followed by hand-drawn sketches and 3D renderings. Once approved, our master jewelers craft your piece using traditional techniques. The custom design process typically takes 4-8 weeks.

How do I care for my jewelry?

Store jewelry separately in soft pouches to prevent scratches. Avoid contact with chemicals, perfumes, and lotions. Clean gently with a soft cloth. For diamonds, use mild soap and warm water with a soft brush. Pearls should only be wiped with a damp cloth. All jewelry comes with detailed care instructions.

What shipping options do you offer?

We offer free insured shipping worldwide on all orders. Domestic orders typically arrive in 3-5 business days, while international shipping takes 7-14 business days. Express shipping (1-2 business days) is available for an additional fee. All packages require signature confirmation and are fully insured.
