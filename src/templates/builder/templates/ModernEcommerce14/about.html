Our Story

Timeless Craftsmanship Since 1925

Founded by master jeweler Giovanni Moretti in Florence, Italy, {brand} began as a small atelier dedicated to the art of fine jewelry making. Giovanni's vision was simple yet profound: to create pieces that would transcend generations, becoming family heirlooms that tell stories of love, celebration, and legacy.

Today, guided by Giovanni's great-granddaughter Sofia Moretti, we continue this tradition of excellence from our New York atelier, blending century-old techniques with contemporary design.

Our Journey Through Time

1925

Humble Beginnings

Giovanni Moretti opens a small atelier in Florence, Italy, specializing in bespoke engagement rings and family crest jewelry for European nobility.

1955

Expansion to New York

Giovanni's son, Marco Moretti, establishes the first American boutique on Fifth Avenue, introducing Italian craftsmanship to the New York elite.

1988

Sustainable Practices

Under Elena Moretti's leadership, we pioneer ethical sourcing initiatives, becoming one of the first jewelry houses to use 100% conflict-free diamonds.

2020

Digital Innovation

Launch of our virtual design studio, allowing clients worldwide to collaborate with our designers in real-time, while maintaining our artisanal approach.

Present Day

Global Recognition

With boutiques in New York, London, and Tokyo, and online presence serving 50+ countries, we continue to honor our heritage while innovating for the future.
