Our Story

A journey of purity, elegance, and transformative skincare since 2020

The Essence of Pure Beauty

Founded by skincare visionary Elena Moreau, LUMIÈRE ÉCLAT was born from a simple belief: true beauty comes from purity, intention, and self-care. What began as a small atelier in Provence has grown into a beloved skincare destination, trusted by those who seek the finest ingredients and transformative results.

Elena's philosophy was revolutionary for its time: harness the power of nature, honor traditional formulations, and deliver results without compromise. Today, guided by her daughter Sofia, we continue this legacy, creating products that nourish not just the skin, but the soul.

"Skincare is self-care. Every ritual is an act of love." - Elena Moreau

Our Journey

2020

A Dream Begins

Elena Moreau opens her first atelier in Provence, France, creating small-batch skincare using local botanicals and traditional French formulation techniques.

2022

Expansion & Recognition

LUMIÈRE ÉCLAT gains international recognition, with our signature Radiance Serum becoming a cult favorite among skincare enthusiasts worldwide.

2024

Sustainable Future

We launch our carbon-neutral initiative, committing to 100% sustainable packaging and ethical sourcing across our entire supply chain.

Today

A Global Community

With a loyal community of over 500,000 customers worldwide, we continue our mission to redefine skincare as an act of self-love and conscious beauty.
