Our Story

Founded in 2010 by interior designer Isabella Moreau, Elysian began as a passionate pursuit of timeless design. What started as a small studio creating bespoke home accessories has evolved into a globally recognized brand, yet we've never lost our commitment to artisanal craftsmanship and personalized service.

Each piece in our collection tells a story of skilled artisans, premium materials, and thoughtful design. We work directly with master craftspeople around the world to bring you accessories that are not only beautiful but built to last for generations.
