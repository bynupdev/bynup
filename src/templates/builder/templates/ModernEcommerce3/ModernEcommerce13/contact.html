Frequently Asked Questions

What is the typical timeline for purchasing a luxury property through Elite Residences?

The timeline for acquiring a luxury property typically ranges from 30 to 90 days, depending on factors such as property availability, due diligence requirements, and financing arrangements. Our concierge team streamlines the entire process, from initial consultation to closing, ensuring a seamless experience. For international clients or complex transactions involving multiple jurisdictions, additional time may be required for legal and regulatory compliance.

Do you offer property viewing services for international clients?

Yes, we specialize in serving international clients and offer comprehensive property viewing services including virtual tours, video consultations, and in-person viewings coordinated with your travel schedule. Our multilingual team can arrange private viewings, provide local market insights, and assist with travel logistics. For clients unable to travel, we offer detailed digital presentations and live virtual tours conducted by our property specialists.

What due diligence services do you provide during the property acquisition process?

Our comprehensive due diligence services include property inspection by certified engineers, title verification, zoning compliance checks, environmental assessments, and historical preservation reviews. We also provide investment analysis, market valuation, and comparative market analysis. For international properties, we coordinate with local legal experts to ensure compliance with all regulatory requirements and provide detailed reports on tax implications and ownership structures.

Can you assist with property management after purchase?

Absolutely. We offer premium property management services including maintenance coordination, staff hiring and management (housekeepers, gardeners, security), utility management, and seasonal preparation. Our concierge services also include rental management for investment properties, with comprehensive tenant screening, lease administration, and regular property inspections. We provide detailed monthly reports and 24/7 emergency support for all managed properties.

What makes Elite Residences different from other luxury real estate agencies?

Elite Residences distinguishes itself through our exclusive focus on ultra-premium properties, personalized concierge service, and global network of luxury specialists. We maintain strict confidentiality, offer access to off-market listings, and provide comprehensive investment analysis. Our team includes former architects, interior designers, and financial analysts who offer holistic advisory services. We also provide post-purchase integration services, helping clients with interior design, art acquisition, and lifestyle curation for their new property.
