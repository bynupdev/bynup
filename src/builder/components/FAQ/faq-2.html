02

Can I really cancel anytime? What's the actual process?

Yes. One click from your account, no phone call, no "retention specialist," no "are you sure?" survey that you have to click through four times. Here's the exact flow:

  • Log into your account → Subscriptions → Cancel
  • One confirmation screen (we ask why so we can improve, but it's skippable)
  • Done. Any pending shipments stop immediately. No further charges.

No retention tactic, no exit discount, no call. We've had exactly 12 cancellations in three years and we've never once followed up to ask them back — because nobody wants that.

You can also skip a delivery (if you're out of town, or have a bag backlog), pause the subscription for up to 6 months, or change the frequency — all from the same dashboard.

03

How fresh is the coffee when it arrives?

We roast Monday, Tuesday, and Thursday mornings. Subscription orders placed by Sunday night roast the following Monday and ship Tuesday — which means your coffee is 2–4 days off roast when it arrives. That's the freshness window where coffee tastes best.

Every bag has a printed roast date on the bottom. If you get a bag that's more than 10 days off roast, we consider that a mistake on our end — send us a photo and we'll ship a fresh replacement at no cost.

Compare: supermarket coffee is typically 4–8 months off roast. Most national brands ship 2–3 weeks off roast. The 48-hour window is genuinely unusual.

04

Do I choose my coffee, or does the subscription pick for me?

You choose. There are two paths:

  • Pick your own: choose a specific coffee from our current lineup, and we ship it on your cadence. If it sells out before your next shipment, we'll email you to swap it for a similar coffee.
  • Roaster's choice: choose a roast level (light / medium / dark) and we ship a rotating selection matched to that profile. This is what 60% of our subscribers do — it's how most people find new favorites.

You can switch between these any time from your account. Most people start with Roaster's choice, then switch to a specific coffee once they discover one they love.

05

What if I don't have a grinder?

We grind to order at no extra cost — choose your brew method at checkout (pour-over, drip, espresso, French press, Aeropress, moka, cold brew) and we'll grind to match. Ground coffee ships within 24 hours of grinding, so it arrives as fresh as pre-ground coffee can.

That said — if you're going to be drinking coffee regularly, a burr grinder is the single biggest upgrade you can make to your cup. Whole bean stays fresher for longer, and you get more control over the brew. We sell a hand burr grinder for $65 that's genuinely good, but any burr grinder from anywhere will work.

06

How does shipping work? Is it free?

Free shipping on subscription orders of $40+. Since most subscribers are on the 2-bag (24oz) plan, this usually works out to free shipping on every order. Single-bag subscriptions are $5 flat shipping.

We ship via USPS Priority Mail (2–3 business days) or UPS Ground (1–5 business days depending on distance from Portland). You can choose your preferred carrier at checkout.

Every shipment includes tracking, and we'll email you the moment your order ships — not just the roast date, but the actual "your coffee is on the way" notification.

07

What if I don't like the coffee?

30-day guarantee, no return needed. If a bag doesn't land — for any reason — email us, tell us what was off, and we'll refund that bag in full. You don't have to send it back. You don't have to explain yourself. Just tell us.

We'd also love to know what you didn't like, because it helps us match you with the right coffee next time. But that's optional — the refund goes through either way.

If it happens twice in a row, we'll proactively reach out to help you pick a different coffee. Because if you're not enjoying the coffee, we'd rather fix that than keep shipping you something you don't like.

Question we didn't answer? Email us.

Ember is six people — the person who reads your email is likely the person who roasted your coffee. Reply time: usually under 4 hours.
