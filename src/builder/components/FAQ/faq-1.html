M

How is this different from the $40 LED mask on Amazon?

Marcus · 3:42 PM
L

The difference is irradiance and wavelength precision. The $40 masks deliver around 15 mW/cm² at unpredictable wavelengths — enough to look like they're doing something, not enough to penetrate to the dermis where collagen lives.

Lumière delivers 32 mW/cm² at 630nm red and 850nm near-infrared — both measured, both clinically studied at those exact values. That's what makes the difference between "spa feeling" and "measurable result."

It's also why we're more expensive. The diodes alone cost more than most masks do.

J

What if I don't see results? Can I actually return it after 60 days?

Jade · 5:11 PM
L

Yes — 60 days, full refund, no return shipping needed. We mean it.

Our return rate is about 4.2% (industry average is 8–15% for skincare devices). We'd rather eat the loss on returns than have customers who felt trapped. You can use the device for 60 days, decide it's not for you, and get every dollar back.

The only thing we ask: use it for at least 4 weeks before deciding. Light therapy takes time — one session won't change anything, and that's how it works for everyone, not just you.

Lumière · 5:14 PM
R

Why is there a countdown timer on the site? That feels sketchy.

Rachel · 6:28 PM
L

Also fair. The timer is real — it's counting down to the end of our current production batch. We manufacture in batches of 1,200 units, and when a batch sells out, the next one is 8–10 weeks out. The timer shows how long the current batch will be available.

We know the format looks like classic dropship urgency. The difference is: if you miss it, you'll actually see the price go up or the product go out of stock for two months. We don't extend the timer, and we don't restart it. It's not a marketing trick — it's a real supply constraint.

If that still feels sketchy to you — totally fair, and we get it. Read the study, use the 60-day guarantee, and decide with actual experience instead of marketing language.
