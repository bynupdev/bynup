Social Media Payment Guide
How It Works:
  1. Customer selects "Social Media Payment" at checkout
  2. They enter their contact and delivery details
  3. System creates a professional order summary
  4. Customer gets links to your social media accounts with pre-filled order
  5. Customer sends the order via their preferred platform
  6. You receive the order directly in your DM/inbox
Tips for Success:
  • Enable multiple platforms to give customers choice
  • Respond promptly to incoming orders
  • Share payment instructions in your response
  • Use the custom template to match your brand voice
  • Test each platform before going live
