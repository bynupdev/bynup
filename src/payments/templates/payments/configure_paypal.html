PayPal Setup Guide
Sandbox Setup (Testing):
  1. Go to: PayPal Developer
  2. Create Sandbox Business Account in Accounts section
  3. Create Sandbox App: Dashboard → My Apps & Credentials → Create App
  4. Copy Credentials: Sandbox Client ID and Secret
  5. Test: Use sandbox personal accounts for testing
Live Setup (Production):
  1. Business Account: Ensure PayPal Business account
  2. Create Live App: In PayPal Developer Dashboard
  3. Get Live Credentials: Live Client ID and Secret
  4. Switch to Live: Disable sandbox mode when ready
  5. Monitor: Check transactions in PayPal Manager
Pro Tips
  • Always test thoroughly in sandbox before going live
  • Keep your client secret secure - never share it
  • Use strong passwords for your PayPal accounts
  • Monitor your PayPal account for suspicious activity
