How It Works:
  1. Customer selects "Pay on Delivery" at checkout
  2. Order is created with status "Pending Pay on Delivery"
  3. You process and deliver the order
  4. Customer pays upon delivery
  5. You confirm payment in admin panel
Best Practices:
  • Set reasonable maximum amounts to minimize risk
  • Use delivery zones for areas you service well
  • Provide clear instructions to customers
  • Confirm payments promptly after delivery
  • Track delivery personnel for accountability
