Frequently asked questions

Are there any transaction fees?

No! Unlike Shopify and others, we don't charge any transaction fees. You keep 100% of what you sell. You only pay your payment provider's standard rates (Stripe, PayPal, etc.).

What counts as a product?

A product is any item you sell in your store. Product variants (like sizes or colors) don't count as separate products. You can have unlimited variants per product.

Can I upgrade or downgrade?

Absolutely. You can change your plan at any time. If you upgrade, you'll be prorated. If you downgrade, the change takes effect at the next billing cycle.

Do I need a credit card to start?

No credit card required for the free plan. You can start building your store right away and upgrade when you're ready to sell more products.

What payment providers can I use?

You can use Stripe, PayPal, or any other payment provider. We don't lock you into a specific processor or charge additional fees.

Is there a long-term contract?

No contracts, no commitments. You can cancel anytime directly from your dashboard. Your store will remain active until the end of your billing period.
